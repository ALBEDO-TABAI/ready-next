---
title: "Notes that remain unfinished"
---

A digital garden can contain unfinished thoughts if their status is visible. I put a short question at the top of a draft and link to the evidence that might change my mind.

An index page is useful when it explains relationships between notes, not just when it lists every filename. Once a month I revisit one old path through the garden and repair links that no longer make sense. Leaving a note unfinished is different from presenting it as settled knowledge.
