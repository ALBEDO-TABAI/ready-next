---
title: "Readwise export workflow"
---

I keep a plain Markdown copy of my reading highlights. Before the weekly export, I check whether the book title and author are consistent with earlier notes. A renamed title can look like a new book in the destination folder.

After export I compare one familiar annotation against the original. New highlights belong with the existing note, while my own comments stay visibly separate from quotations. The untouched export goes into an archive before I edit anything. This is my working checklist, not a promise about the current interface of any service.
