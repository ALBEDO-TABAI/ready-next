---
title: "Newsletter editing checklist"
---

The opening paragraph should tell a busy reader why this issue exists. Three useful links are better than twelve links with the same announcement. I keep a short note about the intended reader beside the draft.

Before sending, I read the subject line on a narrow screen and check every date. The final pass is for broken links, image descriptions, and the plain text version. A correction in next week's issue is preferable to silently pretending an error never happened.
